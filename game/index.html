THE STORY SO FAR

A city worth saving.

Mara Vega has returned to Solara Bay after years away. Her family’s Harbor Garage is days from closing, and the neighborhood is changing faster than anyone can keep up with.

With her friend Nico, Mara takes on local jobs, uncovers who is behind a suspicious waterfront redevelopment deal, and brings the community together. Every completed job opens another part of the city.

Chapter 1 — The First Run: Pick up a box of spare parts from the marina and bring it safely to Harbor Garage.
